# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners and small business owners in the Central Valley, California (Sanger, Fresno, Clovis, and surrounding areas). They're comparing a few local painters, scrolling on their phone or laptop, looking for someone they can trust with their home. They care about quality, reliability, and working with real people, not faceless companies.

## Product Purpose

M5 Painting's website is the front door to a family-owned painting business. It needs to build trust fast, show real work, communicate warmth and competence, and make it effortless to request a free estimate. Success is a visitor picking up the phone or filling out the contact form.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, confident. Like a firm handshake from someone who grew up down the road. Not corporate, not slick, not trying too hard. The confidence comes from doing good work and knowing it.

## Anti-references

- Generic contractor website templates (stock photos, cookie-cutter layouts, "Your Trusted Partner" headings)
- Cold corporate SaaS aesthetics (Inter font, purple gradients, metric dashboards)
- Overly minimalist tech sites that feel empty
- Busy, cluttered small-business sites with too many fonts and colors
- Any design that makes a family painting business look like a Silicon Valley startup

## Design Principles

1. **Show the craft** — Let real photos, real reviews, and real details do the talking. The site should feel like opening the door to a well-run family shop.
2. **Blue is paint** — The brand blue isn't a tech accent color. It's the color of a fresh coat on a wall. Use it boldly, like actual paint on a surface.
3. **Earn trust in seconds** — A visitor should feel "these are real people who do great work" before they scroll.
4. **Vary the rhythm** — Each section earns its own composition. No template repetition.
5. **Confident, not loud** — Bold choices, restrained execution. The design should feel intentional, not desperate for attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Good contrast ratios on all text, especially white-on-blue sections. Readable on mobile. No hover-only functionality. Respect reduced motion preferences.
